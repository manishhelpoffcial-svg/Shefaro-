FAQ

Common Questions

Quick answers to common questions about shipping, tracking, and courier partners.

How can I track my shipment? expand_more
Enter your AWB Number or Reference ID on the Tracking page to check your shipment status in real-time.
What information do I need to contact support? expand_more
Keep your AWB Number or Reference ID ready when contacting SheFaro support. It helps our agents quickly identify your shipment and resolve queries.
Can individuals send parcels through SheFaro? expand_more
Yes. SheFaro is designed for both individual shippers sending single parcels as well as businesses shipping at scale.
What courier partners are available? expand_more
SheFaro currently supports Amazon, DTDC, Delhivery, Shadowfax, eKart and XpressBees. Blue Dart is planned as an upcoming courier partner.
What shipment weight does SheFaro support? expand_more
SheFaro supports shipments starting from 500g up to 20kg across domestic express and standard lines.
